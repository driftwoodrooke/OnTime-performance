OnTime Performance — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

OnTime Performance does not collect, store, or share personal information.

Information We Collect

OnTime Performance does not require an account and does not collect personal information, location data, contacts, or usage analytics.

Data Storage

Departure times are stored locally on your device so that the app can remember your selected departure time. This information is not sent to us or shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

OnTime Performance does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support information provided on the App Store.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
